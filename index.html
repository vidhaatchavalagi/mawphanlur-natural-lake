A QUIET CORNER OF MEGHALAYA

A Quiet Corner of Meghalaya

Tucked away in the undulating highlands of West Khasi Hills, Mawphanlur Natural Lake Guesthouse is a peaceful sanctuary where cool mountain air and mirror-calm waters replace the noise of modern life.

Surrounded by seven pristine natural lakes and rolling grassy mounds reminiscent of the Scottish Highlands, the guesthouse offers a quiet place to unwind, breathe deeply, and experience the untouched serenity of Meghalaya.

How to Reach Us

1
From Shillong (~70 km / 2.5 hrs)

Drive west along the Shillong–Nongstoin Highway (NH 106) past Mairang toward Markasa.

2
From Markasa (~5 km)

At Markasa junction, take the dedicated uphill road signposted for Mawphanlur Natural Lake.
